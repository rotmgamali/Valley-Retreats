The Rhythm of the Retreat

A typical day in the valley

Awakening

The sun hits the peaks. You rise with the light. A warm tonic is delivered to your door. The valley is silent, save for the birds. You begin with guided breathwork on your deck.

Nourishment & Expansion

After a farm-to-table brunch, the day opens up. You hike the ridgeline, join a foraging workshop, or simply rest in the sauna. The body is fed, the mind is clear.

Integration

As dusk falls, we gather. Sometimes for a sound bath, sometimes for a fire ceremony. We integrate the day's lessons under the vast blanket of stars.
