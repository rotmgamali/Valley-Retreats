A Guide to the Valley Night Sky

Nature

Ogden Valley is adjacent to North Fork Park, one of the world's few certified International Dark Sky Parks. Here, the Milky Way isn't a faint idea; it is a ribbon of light that stretches horizon to horizon.

We provide telescopes in every yurt for guests to explore the cosmos. But the best way to see the stars is simply to lie back on the grass (or snow) and look up.

This month, look for Orion rising in the east and the Seven Sisters (Pleiades) sparkling high overhead. It is a humbling reminder of our place in the universe.
