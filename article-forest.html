The Science of Forest Bathing

Wellness
Shinrin-yoku, or "forest bathing," is not just a poetic concept; it is a scientifically proven therapy. Originating in Japan in the 1980s, the practice involves immersing oneself in nature with mindful intention.

Research shows that trees release phytoncides, airborne chemicals that protect them from insects. When we breathe these in, our bodies respond by increasing the number and activity of a type of white blood cell called "natural killer" cells, which kill tumor- and virus-infected cells in our bodies.

At Valley Retreats, we take this practice deeply. Our guides help you slow down, engage your senses, and truly breathe in the medicine of the Ogden Valley pines. It is more than a walk in the woods; it is a prescription for longevity.
