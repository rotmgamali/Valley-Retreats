In the Kitchen with Chef Marco

Culinary • Philosophy • Taste

F

"The tomato should taste like the sun. The mushroom should taste like the damp earth." Chef Marco repeats this mantra as he chops rainbow chard in the open-air kitchen of Valley Retreats. For him, cooking is not about manipulation; it is about revelation.


Plant-Forward, Not deprivation

Our menu is vegetarian, but we dislike the label. It implies something missing—meat. Instead, we focus on abundance. When you roast a carrot in salt crust for four hours, it takes on a meaty, umami richness that rivals any steak. When you use fermentation to age cashew cheese, it develops a funk and depth that honors the tradition of dairy.


"We don't just feed the body. We feed the microbiome, the mood, and the spirit."


Local sourcing

90% of what we serve comes from within 50 miles. The honey is from the hives on the south ridge. The trout (for those who request it) is from the Weber river. This means the menu changes daily. Adaptability is the primary skill in our kitchen. We cook what the land gives us, when it gives it.
