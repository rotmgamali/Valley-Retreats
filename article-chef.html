In the Kitchen with Chef Marco

Culinary

"Food is energy," says Chef Marco. "When you eat something that was pulled from the earth hours ago, you feel that vitality."

Our culinary philosophy is simple: let the ingredients speak. We work with local farmers in Eden and Huntsville to source the freshest organic produce. Our menu changes daily, dictated not by a schedule, but by the harvest.

In this interview, Marco shares his favorite winter root vegetable recipes and explains why mindful eating is a form of meditation in itself.
