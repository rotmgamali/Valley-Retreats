Foraging in Ogden Valley

Nature

The land provides, if we know how to look. The slopes of the Wasatch Range are teeming with edible treasures, from wild onions and elderberries to elusive morel mushrooms.

Our culinary team bases their menus on what the valley offers. But we also invite guests to join us in the gathering process. Learning to identify the food beneath your feet changes the way you walk through the world. It transforms the landscape from a backdrop into a pantry, a garden, and a friend.

Note: Always forage with a guide to ensure safety and sustainability.
